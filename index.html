Key Updates

OBBBA: 100% Bonus Depreciation Permanent

October 2026 | Federal
Tax | Real Estate

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act made 100% bonus depreciation permanent for qualifying commercial real estate components acquired after January 19, 2025.

✓ Action: Review current real estate component depreciation methods; consider cost segregation studies for maximum benefit

Section 179 Limit Doubled to $2.56M

January 2026 | Federal
Tax | Equipment

The Section 179 expensing limit jumped from $1.25M to $2.56M for 2026, with phase-out beginning at $4.09M in total purchases.

✓ Action: Maximize equipment purchases; pair with 100% bonus depreciation for full year-one expensing

QOZ Critical Deadline: December 31, 2026

October 2026 | Federal
Tax | Investment

Investors who deferred gains in Opportunity Zones must recognize that deferred gain by December 31, 2026, unless rolling into post-2026 investments under new rules.

⚠️ Action: Review all QOZ positions immediately; determine if extension or new rollover needed before year-end

MA Surtax Threshold Increased to $1.1078M

October 2026 | Massachusetts
Tax | State

The Massachusetts millionaire's surtax threshold rose to $1,107,750 from $1,083,150, with 4% surtax applying to income above this level (combined 9% top rate).

✓ Action: Adjust tax planning strategies; review income deferral and entity structure for high earners

MA Commercial Conversion Tax Credit: $15.3M Awarded

October 2026 | Massachusetts
Tax Credit | Housing

Governor Healey announced $15.3M in Commercial Conversion Tax Credit awards for 856 new homes across Boston, Dedham, Springfield, Westford, and Worcester.

✓ Action: Monitor EOHLC for next funding round; evaluate vacant commercial properties for conversion eligibility

Hard Money Lending: Rates & Usury Status Unchanged

October 2026 | Massachusetts
Regulatory | Lending

Massachusetts allows interest rates up to 20% with Attorney General notice; no new usury law changes in 2026. Default legal rate remains 6% unless contracted higher in writing.

✓ Action: Continue existing notification protocols; no operational changes required
